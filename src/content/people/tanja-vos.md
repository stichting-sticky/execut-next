Tanja Vos is Professor of Software Engineering at the Open Universiteit in the Netherlands and at the Universitat Politècnica de València in Spain.

She has over 25 years of experience in teaching and research, with a strong focus on advancing the field of software testing. Tanja has been involved in numerous research projects on software testing in industrial contexts and has coordinated several EU-funded projects in this area.

Through her work in Erasmus and Leonardo initiatives, she has actively contributed to strengthening collaboration between academia and industry. Her research and practical experience have led to close cooperation with companies on automated testing in real-world environments.

Tanja is the project lead of TESTAR.org, an open-source initiative aimed at developing intelligent tools for automated GUI-level software testing.
