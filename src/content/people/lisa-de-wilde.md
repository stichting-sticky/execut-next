Lisa de Wilde is a Security Consultant with a background in Computer Science (BSc) and Cyber Security (MSc).

She specializes in monitoring, detection, and incident response, and has supported dozens of organizations in handling and resolving security incidents and crises. Based on this hands-on experience, Lisa also advises organizations on how to better prevent and prepare for cyber incidents.

In 2023, she founded her own company, Cyber Radiant, to help even more organizations improve their cybersecurity. Alongside her consultancy work, Lisa is an active public speaker who shares real-world incident response experiences through podcasts, webinars, events, and radio appearances.

Lisa looks forward to this event and to sharing insights into what happens inside an organization when it is hit by a cyber attack.
