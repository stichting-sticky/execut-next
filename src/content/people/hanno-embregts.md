Hanno Embregts is a Java Developer with a strong passion for learning, teaching, and making music.

In his day-to-day role as a Teacher and Technology Advocate at Info Support, Hanno enjoys fast-paced and versatile work. He combines Java development, software architecture, public speaking, leading Info Support's Speaker Community, and teaching courses at the Info Support Knowledge Centre.

Hanno is a Java Champion, an Oracle ACE Pro, and one of the leaders of the Dutch Java User Group (NLJUG).

Outside of work, Hanno enjoys making music with friends. He plays the flute and guitar and enjoys singing. Software conferences are his favorite place to be, as they allow him to combine learning, teaching, and occasionally making music.
