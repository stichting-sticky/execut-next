1+1=2, right?

We teach students to test with the same mindset as programming. That doesn't work. Testing is not confirmation of what we already think we know. It is investigating what we don't know. It is working in uncertainty, dealing with assumptions, and giving meaning to unexpected behavior.

In this talk, I will show why current testing education is fundamentally misaligned with practice&mdash;and why that is becoming dangerous in the age of AI systems. Using concrete examples, puzzles, and experiences from education, I will present an alternative: testing as an empirical, investigative skill.
