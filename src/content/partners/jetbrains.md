JetBrains creates intelligent software development tools and collaboration solutions trusted by over 11 million users worldwide. Since 2000, they have built a portfolio of more than 30 products, including leading IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, and GoLand, as well as team tools like YouTrack, TeamCity, and Datalore. JetBrains is also the creator of Kotlin, the officially preferred language for Android development. Their mission is to make professional software development more productive and enjoyable by helping developers automate routine tasks, focus on meaningful challenges, and build high-quality software throughout every stage of the development process."
