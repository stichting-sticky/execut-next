The Ministry of Defence of the Netherlands is the government department responsible for national defence and the Dutch armed forces. It works to safeguard the freedom, peace, and security of the Netherlands and its allies, defending national territory, contributing to international stability, and supporting civil authorities in crises and emergencies.
