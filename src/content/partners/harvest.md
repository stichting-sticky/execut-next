Harvest BV is a Dutch tech talent accelerator that runs a highly selective Post-Master program for software, data, and business analysts. Each year they select top graduates (“cream of the crop”) and combine real work placements at leading companies with intensive training, masterclasses, and coaching to rapidly develop technical and professional skills. Harvest helps ambitious young professionals make a strong start in IT and supports organizations in building high-quality engineering teams.
